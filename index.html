Good questions deserve clear answers

Frequently asked questions.

Is Maya a real person?

No. Maya is a fictional AI companion persona. KINDRA is being designed to offer thoughtful AI conversation without presenting an AI as a human being.

Can I chat with Maya on this website today?

Not as a live generative service. The public website is an introduction. Separate offline engineering demonstrations use clearly labelled scripted or fixture responses.

Will KINDRA remember everything I say?

That is not the intended design. Relationship Graph™ is being built around explicit approval of individual memories, with correction and deletion controls. These controls are not yet available as a production customer service.

Is KINDRA available on mobile?

The public website is designed for mobile browsers. A complete authenticated customer app and live companion experience are still in development.

Can I subscribe to KINDRA?

Not yet. Prices displayed on this site are proposed USD planning figures, not available subscriptions. Payment, entitlements and operating costs must be validated before launch.

Will there be voice or video conversations?

Voice and video are part of the longer-term vision. Neither is available as a live public KINDRA feature, and both require separate privacy, safety, quality and cost validation.

How will privacy and safety work?

Our goal is clear consent, meaningful memory controls, transparent AI identification and strong safeguards. Authentication, age assurance, moderation, security and applicable privacy compliance must be completed before external customer use.
